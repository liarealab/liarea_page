LIAREA launches Hackathon on burned area scar detection using remote sensing data, in WorCap 2025

  • by: Miranda, Mateus
  • date: Ago 28, 2025
  • São José dos Campos, Brazil

WorCap 2025 invites students, researchers, and professionals in the fields of data science, remote sensing, and artificial intelligence to take part in its upcoming Hackathon themed: Detection of Burned Area Scars Using Remote Sensing Data

Hackathon flyer.

This initiative is organized by the Laboratory of Artificial Intelligence for Aerospace and Environmental Applications (LIAREA/INPE) and aims to foster the development of innovative solutions using machine learning techniques and satellite imagery analysis to identify burned area scars.

  • Registration period: August 18–31
  • 📌 Platforms: Kaggle & Discord
  • 📌 Competition period: September 1–7
  • 📌 Closing session & award ceremony: September 9

Solutions will be evaluated according to Performance, Reproducibility, and Innovation. The best teams will be recognized during the closing session and awarded with certificates and exclusive WorCAP kits. For further details, please visit the competition page.
