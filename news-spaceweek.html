LIAREA Participates in Space Week Nordeste 2025

  • by: Costa, João
  • date: Sep 9, 2025
  • São José dos Campos, Brazil

From September 15 to 21, 2025, Space Week Nordeste 2025 was held in Recife, Brazil. The event featured mini-courses, lectures, and roundtable discussions exploring the future of space exploration, bringing together experts, researchers, students, and leading companies in the field.

The Laboratory of Artificial Intelligence for Aerospace and Environmental Applications (LIAREA) at INPE took part in the event with the presentation of Short Course 8 – Artificial Intelligence, Space, and Environment. In addition, Rafael Santos, coordinator of PGCAP/INPE and a member of LIAREA, presented the lecture “Data Science, Space Big Data, and Artificial Intelligence.”

In the photo below is also Prof. Antonio Geraldo Ferreira (EOLLAB/UFC), the general coordinator of Space Week Nordeste 2025.

Valdivino Santiago, Rafael Santos, and Prof. Antonio Geraldo Ferreira at Space Week Nordeste 2025, Recife, Brazil.
