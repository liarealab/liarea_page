LIAREA's 2nd Workshop integrates INPE programs and promotes connections between several agents

  • by: Costa, B. C.
  • date: Jun 15, 2026
  • São José dos Campos, Brazil

LIAREA's 2nd Workshop took place on June 11th and 12th and included the participation of INPE laboratory Intelligent Modeling, Predicting, and Analytics for Climate Threats (IMPACT) in partnership with the National Center for Monitoring and Alerts for Natural Disasters (CEMADEN), as well other INPE programs such as BiomasBR and its TerraClass project.

The full and a half day presentations were spoken by postgraduate students, INPE collaborators and external partners, which addressed many fields, such as:

  1. Remote Sensing;
  2. Weather and Climate Forecasting;
  3. Computer Vision and drones;
  4. Large Language Models;
  5. Astronomy and Astrophysics;
  6. Artificial Intelligence and Internet of Things (AIoT).

The workshop was also supported by the Brazilian Space Agency (AEB) and was streamed in YouTube (recordings for the first and the second day can be found in the hyperlinks). Program details are found here.
