Workshop on Deep Learning for Remote Sensing Applications to be Held on May 19

  • by: Miranda, M.
  • date: March 31, 2025
  • São José dos Campos, Brazil

The Laboratory of Artificial Intelligence for Aerospace and Environmental Applications (LIAREA) is excited to announce a workshop “Introduction to Deep Learning in Remote Sensing Applications.” It’s happening on May 19th from 4:50 PM to 6:20 PM. This event is part of the GEOINFO2025 initiative. It’s a great chance to learn the basics of using deep learning for remote sensing.

Whether you're a student, researcher, or just curious about AI, this session is your chance to dive into the world of deep learning for remote sensing. This workshop is designed by Rafael Marinho de Andrade, Pedro Vinicius da Silva Brito, Mateus de Souza Miranda, and Valdivino Alexandre de Santiago Júnior, and aims to introduce the cutting-edge methodologies and how they are transforming aerospace and environmental monitoring—with real-world insights and hands-on cases using Deep Leearning models.
