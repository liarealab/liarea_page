LIAREA Laboratory Expansion Event – First Official Meeting

  • by:Santiago, V.
  • date:March 16, 2025
  • São José dos Campos, Brazil

The LIAREA Laboratory celebrated the official inauguration of its expansion with its first official meeting. The event gathered research coordinators from the institution, highlighting the laboratory's commitment to advancing artificial intelligence (AI) in aerospace and environmental applications.

A special highlight of the event was the award ceremony for the best logo design, presented to Mateus de Souza Miranda , recognizing his outstanding contribution to the lab’s visual identity. The award was handed over by Prof. Dr. Valdivino Santiago and Prof. Dr. Rafael Santos , acknowledging his creative work and dedication to LIAREA.

The event also welcomed the majority of Applied Computing students from the institution, further strengthening the connection between members. It served as a valuable opportunity to foster collaboration and unity among researchers and students, reinforcing LIAREA’s mission of innovation and teamwork.
