LIAREA participates in CSBC 2025 with presentation on seasonal precipitation forecasting

  • by: Santiago, Valdivino
  • date: Ago 6, 2025
  • São José dos Campos, Brazil

The 45th edition of the Brazilian Computer Society Congress (CSBC 2025) took place from July 20 to 24 in Maceió, Alagoas, bringing together the national computing research community in one of Brazil’s most prominent academic events.

The Laboratory of Artificial Intelligence for Aerospace and Environmental Applications (LIAREA), from the National Institute for Space Research (INPE), participated in the event with the presentation of the paper “Exploring Deep Learning Techniques for Seasonal Prediction of Autumn Precipitation in South America”, during the 16th Workshop on Applied Computing for the Environment and Natural Resources Management (WCAMA 2025).

Dr. Valdivino in front the CSBC backdrop.

The study is part of the master’s research developed by Matheus Corrêa Domingos, a student in INPE’s Graduate Program in Applied Computing (PGCAP), and is co-authored by Juliana Anochi and Elcio Hideiti Shiguemori.

LIAREA’s participation in CSBC 2025 highlights its ongoing commitment to advancing artificial intelligence solutions to address environmental and climate-related challenges, contributing to impactful scientific progress in Brazil.
